OUR HERITAGE

Bottled in the City of Kings

Balaji Water is bottled at our facility on NH39, near Bhadarwar, in Mauranipur — a town whose royal past inspires the standard we bottle to. Every batch passes through advanced multi-stage filtration before it ever leaves our doors.
